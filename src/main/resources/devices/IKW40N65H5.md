# Infineon IKW40N65H5

The data describe the insulated-gate bipolar transistor (IGBT). The 25 °C, 40 A voltage drop is the typical saturation voltage in the static characteristics table. The other voltage points are approximate readings from Figure 7 at a 15 V gate voltage.

Figure 12 gives the approximate switching energies at 150 °C. The DC link is 400 V. The gate resistance is 15 Ω. The gate drive is 0 V to 15 V.
