# Wolfspeed C3M0065090D

This is a silicon carbide MOSFET. The conduction points use approximate on-resistance values from Figure 5 at a 15 V gate voltage. The voltage drop is the drain current times the on-resistance. The zero-current point follows this relation.

Figure 24 gives the approximate switching energies for a 400 V DC link at 25 °C. The external gate resistance is 2.5 Ω. The gate drive is −4 V to +15 V. The freewheel device is another C3M0065090D.
