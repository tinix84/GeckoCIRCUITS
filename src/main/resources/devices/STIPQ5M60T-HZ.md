# STMicroelectronics STIPQ5M60T-HZ

The data describe one silicon MOSFET in the inverter module.

The 25 °C, 2.5 A sample uses the typical 0.8 Ω value in Table 6. Thus, the voltage drop is 2.5 A × 0.8 Ω = 2 V. The other conduction samples are approximate points from Figure 12 at a 15 V control supply.

The 25 °C, 2.5 A switching sample comes from Table 7. The other switching samples are approximate points from Figures 15 and 16. The DC link is 300 V. The module uses its internal gate drive.
